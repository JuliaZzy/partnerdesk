2026 DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

This Distributor Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 2026-10-01 by and between Red Bull ("Company") and 联采贸易有限公司 ("Distributor"). A signatory is a "Party". Together they are the "Parties".
The Distributor is the same distributor already appointed for other brands. This Agreement covers only the Red Bull products listed below. It does not change any agreement between the Distributor and another brand.
This file is a fictional agreement prepared for upload testing. Product names come from the catalog. Case prices and minimum order quantities are the figures stored for this brand.

RECITALS

Company supplies the Products and wishes to sell them to the Distributor for resale.
The Distributor wants the exclusive right to market and sell the Products in the Territory, either in its own business or to retailers and other customers.
The Parties agree as follows.

Article 1 Definitions

The recitals are part of this Agreement. The following meanings apply to the singular and the plural.
1.1 Affiliate
"Affiliate" means a person that directly or indirectly controls a Party, is controlled by a Party, or is under common control with a Party, and a director or officer of that person. Control means power over more than 50 percent of the voting shares, or power to direct management, whether by shares, contract, or otherwise.
1.2 Effective Date
"Effective Date" means 2026-10-01, the date the Parties sign this Agreement.
1.3 Person
"Person" means an individual, partnership, company, trust, joint venture, or other entity, and a government or its agency.
1.4 Product
"Product" and "Products" mean the goods supplied by Company under this Agreement. They are the eight catalog SKUs in Article 8, and no other goods.
1.5 Contract Year
"Contract Year" means each period of twelve months beginning on 2026-10-01. The first Contract Year runs from 2026-10-01 through 2027-09-30.
1.6 Shipping Container
"Shipping container" means one purchase order the Distributor has submitted. Each submitted purchase order counts as one shipping container. The count restarts at the first container in each Contract Year. The basis is shipping container sequence, and the period is each contract year.
1.7 Initial Term
"Initial Term" has the meaning in Article 6.
1.8 Successive Term
"Successive Term" has the meaning in Article 6.
1.9 Territory
"Territory" means mainland China and Hong Kong. The allowed country codes are CN and HK.

Article 2 Authority of the Distributor

2.1 Organization
The Distributor is a company organized under the law of the People's Republic of China and has authority to sign this Agreement.
2.2 Licenses
The Distributor will hold the licenses and permits required for the business this Agreement describes. Those licenses stay in the Distributor's name unless a written approval says otherwise.
2.3 Other brands
The Distributor already sells goods of other brands. Those appointments do not breach this Agreement. The Distributor will not sign another agreement that gives a third party the exclusive right to sell the Products in the Territory.

Article 3 Grant of Distribution

3.1 Grant
From the Effective Date, and for the term in Article 6, Company grants the Distributor the exclusive right to market and sell the Products in the Territory.
3.2 Exclusivity
The exclusivity type is exclusive.
The allowed countries are CN and HK. The territory text is mainland China and Hong Kong, exclusive.
Company will not, in the Territory and during the term, sell the Products itself or appoint another distributor for the Products. No other territory is included. A sale outside the Territory, without Company's prior written approval, is a breach.
3.3 No reproduction
The Distributor will not copy, reproduce, or reverse engineer a Product or its formula, and will not give samples to anyone for that purpose. A breach of this Section is a material breach. Company may terminate this Agreement, seek an injunction, and recover damages.
3.4 No alteration
The Distributor will not alter, dilute, or repack a Product, except under written instructions Company issues for that Product.
3.5 Brand name
The Distributor will sell the Products only under the Red Bull name shown in the catalog, unless Company gives prior written approval for a private label. A private-label approval does not change the price or the minimum order quantity unless the approval says so.
3.6 Trademarks
Company grants the Distributor the right, during the term, to advertise and sell the Products with the trademarks that appear on them. That right is a license to use, not a transfer of ownership. This Agreement does not assign any patent, trademark, or other intellectual property. No sale or import gives the Distributor ownership of those rights.
3.7 No other connection
The Distributor will not tell a customer that the Products are the same as, or are approved by, any other brand the Distributor carries.

Article 4 Obligations of the Distributor

4.1 Sales
The Distributor will use the Products in its own business or sell them to customers in the Territory who want them.
4.2 Marketing copies
On Company's request, the Distributor will send a copy of marketing material used for the Products. The Distributor obtains, at its own cost, the licenses and registrations needed to sell. Company does not thereby transfer those licenses to itself.
4.3 Fair selling
The Distributor will not make a false or misleading statement about the Products, and will not use a deceptive trade practice. The Distributor pays the taxes, duties, and tariffs on its own importation and resale.
4.4 Compliance
The Distributor will comply with laws that apply to its import, storage, labeling, advertising, sale, and data handling of the Products in the Territory.
4.5 Expenses
The Distributor pays its own overhead, including staff, marketing, administration, legal fees, and accounting, except where this Agreement says Company pays.
4.6 Storage
The Distributor will store the Products in a clean, safe place, under conditions suitable for a canned or bottled energy drink, until they are sold.
4.7 Reports and forecasts
Within fifteen days after each month ends, the Distributor will send a written report of that month's sales of the Products in the Territory.
At least twenty days before the ship date of an order, the Distributor will send a written forecast for the following two months, so Company can plan supply.
A missed report is a material breach. Company may terminate under Article 9 and may withdraw the container discounts in Article 6 for orders placed after the failure, until the missing report is delivered.
4.8 Insurance
Where product-liability insurance is available in the Territory, the Distributor will insure its activities under this Agreement at its own cost, at a level usual for this trade or as Company reasonably requires, and will name Company as an additional insured when the insurer allows it.

Article 5 Obligations of Company

5.1 Training and data sheets
Company will give reasonable technical support and the product information it has. Company does not have to disclose formulas or manufacturing processes.
Company will make available a technical data sheet for each Product, when Company has one.
Company will give the Distributor an initial explanation of how the Products are sold and stored, and a follow-up if a Product in Article 8 changes.
5.2 Marketing materials
Company will provide marketing materials in electronic form. During the term, the Distributor may use Company's logos for the Products, only to advertise those Products, and only in the Territory.
5.3 Manuals
If Company lends a marketing or operations manual, the manual remains Company's property. The Distributor will return it within five business days after this Agreement ends.
5.4 Later catalog items
If Company adds a new Red Bull catalog item and both Parties sign a written addition to Article 8, that item becomes a Product. Until that writing is signed, the Products are only the eight SKUs in Article 8.
5.5 Risk of loss
Company bears the risk of loss or damage until the goods are received at the port of arrival in mainland China. Company will insure that leg with its carrier.
After the container lock is opened at that port, the Distributor has seventy-two hours to record damaged goods with time-stamped photographs, and seventy-two hours from the opening of the lock to present the claim to Company. The claim must include the evidence this Section requires. If the Distributor does not, Company is not liable for that damage.
If Company accepts a claim for packaging damage, Company will replace the same quantity of damaged goods and will pay the freight, duty, and VAT on the replacement to the extent those amounts were paid on the damaged goods and are not recovered. Company will ship the replacement as soon as it reasonably can.

Article 6 Term, Payment, Discount, and Targets

6.1 Initial Term
The Initial Term starts on the Effective Date, 2026-10-01, and ends on 2031-09-30. That period is five years.
6.2 Successive Term
A Successive Term of five years starts only if, before 2031-09-30, both Parties sign a written schedule of yearly sales targets for that period and the Distributor is not in breach. Silence does not renew this Agreement. If that writing is not signed, the Agreement ends on 2031-09-30.
6.3 Payment
The Distributor pays 50 percent of an order when the order is placed. The Distributor pays the balance within thirty days after the container arrives in mainland China.
The discounted case price in Section 6.4 is available only while the Distributor is in compliance with this Agreement. After a material breach that is still uncured, later orders are at the full Article 8 price.
6.4 Container discount
The discount is calculated on the sequence of submitted purchase orders in the current Contract Year. Each submitted purchase order counts as one shipping container.
The counting period is each contract year. The basis is shipping container sequence.
Orders submitted before 2026-10-01 are not counted in the first Contract Year. When a new Contract Year starts, the sequence starts again at the first container.
The first shipping container and the second shipping container in a Contract Year have a discount of 0 percent. The Distributor pays the Article 8 case price.
The third shipping container and the fourth shipping container in that Contract Year have a discount of 5 percent off the Article 8 case price.
The fifth shipping container, and each shipping container after it in that Contract Year, has a discount of 10 percent off the Article 8 case price.
There is no separate per-SKU discount. A change to a case price applies only to orders placed after both Parties sign a replacement of Article 8. Company will not change a price on an order already submitted.
6.5 Annual sales targets
The annual sales target is measured in USD at the Article 8 case prices, before the container discount. The currency is USD.
Contract Year 1, from 2026-10-01 through 2027-09-30, has an annual sales target of 400000 USD.
Contract Year 2, from 2027-10-01 through 2028-09-30, has an annual sales target of 600000 USD.
Contract Year 3, from 2028-10-01 through 2029-09-30, has an annual sales target of 800000 USD.
Contract Year 4, from 2029-10-01 through 2030-09-30, has an annual sales target of 1000000 USD.
Contract Year 5, from 2030-10-01 through 2031-09-30, has an annual sales target of 1200000 USD.
If a Contract Year exceeds its target, 50 percent of the excess, measured the same way, is subtracted from the next Contract Year's target. The excess does not change the container discounts.
If the Distributor misses a target by 20 percent or more, it will send a written explanation and a plan for the next Contract Year. If Company, acting reasonably, rejects that plan in writing, Company may terminate this Agreement under Article 9 and may buy the Distributor's remaining Products at the Distributor's documented landed cost.

Article 9 Termination

9.1 Notice
Company may terminate this Agreement by thirty days' written notice if any of the following occurs.
The Distributor fails to cure a breach within the time this Agreement gives for a cure.
The Distributor commits a material breach, including a breach of Section 3.3, Section 3.4, Section 3.5, or Section 4.7.
The Distributor makes an assignment for creditors or a bankruptcy petition is filed against it and is not dismissed within thirty days.
The Distributor, or an Affiliate acting for it, takes an action that impairs Company's intellectual property in the Products.
The Distributor sells in the Territory, without Company's prior written consent, another energy drink positioned as a substitute for the Products. Selling other, non-substituting brands is not this event.
9.2 Marks after termination
When this Agreement ends, the Distributor's license to use the trademarks ends. The Distributor will not use or register a mark that is identical or confusingly similar to the Products' marks.
If the Distributor filed a trademark application in China for the Products, it will assign that application, and any registration that issued from it, back to Company. It will sign the papers the China National Intellectual Property Administration requires and will finish the assignment steps within thirty days after Company's written request.
9.3 Remaining stock
Within five working days after termination, the Distributor will send a list of finished goods, packaging, and marketing materials that bear the Products' marks.
Company may, by written choice, buy that stock at the Distributor's documented landed cost, name another person to receive it, or require supervised destruction. The Distributor will deliver or destroy it within fifteen working days after Company confirms the choice.
Purchase orders that Company already accepted stay in force. The container count includes only purchase orders submitted before termination.

Article 10 Private Label, Confidentiality, and Promotion

10.1 Private label
The Distributor may private-label a Product only with Company's prior written approval. Labels and any safety sheet the Distributor issues must follow Company's reasonable directions and the law of the place of sale.
10.2 Confidentiality
A Party may tell its own senior officers the terms of this Agreement. Those officers may not pass the terms to a third party. The Distributor will not reveal a formula, or any information Company marks as proprietary, to a third party.
A breach of this Section causes harm that damages may not fully cover. Company may seek an injunction.
This duty lasts for two years after the Agreement ends. The prices, discounts, and targets in Articles 6, 7, and 8 may be used inside each Party's own business to perform this Agreement.
10.3 Promotional materials
The Distributor may prepare marketing material for the Products if it stays consistent with Company's description of them. Company may reject material that is inaccurate. The Distributor will stop using rejected material.
